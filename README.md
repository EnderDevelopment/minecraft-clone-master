# CloneMaster

CloneMaster is a Minecraft mod that allows players to create clones of themselves using a special item. When you hold the CloneMaster item and right-click, it will clone your character, including your nametag, inventory, armor, and held items. The clones will mimic your actions, attacking whatever you attack and being attacked by whatever attacks you. This mod is designed for personal use in your own Minecraft world.

## Install

1. Download the CloneMaster mod file.
2. Place the downloaded file in the `mods` folder of your Minecraft instance.
3. Launch Minecraft and ensure the mod is loaded correctly.

## Use

1. Obtain the CloneMaster item from the Creative Mode tab.
2. Hold the CloneMaster item in your hand.
3. Right-click to create a clone of yourself.
4. Your clone will mimic your actions, including attacking and being attacked.
5. To remove a clone, simply attack it.
